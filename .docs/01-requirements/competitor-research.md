## 2. YelloTalk (main reference app)

### What it is
- An anonymous social app by Ookbee U: feed and posts, private chat invites, community "campuses", and live audio rooms with paid gifts, coins and a premium plan (S01, S02).
- Aimed at school and university students and young workers who are lonely or want advice (S01). The website leans on university life and campus Community groups for sports, games, films and meals (S03).
- Popular with Gen Z; a 2024 study of 350 users found satisfaction comes from social self-image, relationship support, enjoyment, cost and app quality (S04).
- Ratings are low: 2.1★ on Google Play (about 3.1K reviews), 2.4★ on the App Store (1.8K ratings) (S01, S02).

### What it offers for arranging activities
- Campus Community groups for activities such as sports or meals (S03), and posts plus private chat to talk about them (S01).
- No feature for an activity with a date, place, number of places or a join button is described on the listings or website (S01–S03).
- One older review recalls the app once ran activities that later stopped, and that the same few people kept joining (S01, review 24 Nov 2019, 5★).

### What users report (S01, 140 Google Play reviews)

Rough keyword count over the 140 reviews read (a review can match more than one theme; 15 of the 140 are from 2025–2026):

| Theme | Reviews | Example (paraphrased; date, stars) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crashes, freezes, live-room drops | 42 | Audio drops when switching apps (several, 2018–2026) |
| Bans and mass reporting | 29 | Reported by a group for a differing opinion and banned with no review (11 Feb 2026, 2★; 9 Sep 2026, 1★) |
| Notifications missing or late | 19 | Notifications never arrive although enabled (9 May 2025, 1★) |
| Login problems (Facebook-only sign-in) | 17 | Cannot sign in because only Facebook login is offered and it fails (24 Jun 2024, 1★) |
| No way to contact admins or appeal | 13 | Unlocking a banned account means messaging admins on Facebook; asks for an in-app appeal (25 Dec 2022, 1★) |
| Sexual content, solicitation, scams | 8 | Pulled into private groups and sent sexual messages; cannot block the sender (17 Dec 2023, 1★) |
| Made friends through the app | 8 | Found friends and gaming partners (18 Mar 2020, 5★) |
| Wants nearby or campus scope | 5 | Asks for a nearby-area feature because the public feed is too broad to find friends (11 Feb 2019, 4★) |
| Arranging to meet up | 4 | Arranged to meet someone to go out and was banned because the word was read as sexual (17 Mar 2019, 3★) |
| Account deletion does not work | 2 | Asked to delete the account, months later could still log in with all data (4 Nov 2024, 1★) |

App Store reviews (S02) add: a request that posts show which campus the writer belongs to (18 Apr 2019), and an objection to splitting campuses (16 Nov 2017).

Data handling (S01 data-safety label, as declared by the developer): may share location and personal data with third parties; data is **not** encrypted; users can ask for deletion. The listing says location permission is used for ads.

### Lessons for JoinGun
- **Keep:** a student-only, campus-focused community; easy posting; friends found through shared interests.
- **Avoid:** anonymity without accountability, bans driven only by report counts, no in-app appeal, Facebook-only login, unreliable notifications, deletion that does not happen, unencrypted data.

## 3. Facebook and X

### Facebook
- Events support in-person events with a location, an audience setting chosen at creation (cannot be changed later), invitations, responses and event notifications (S07, S08).
- The help pages checked do not mention a guest limit or "places left", or any check that attendees are students (S07, S08).
- Facebook groups were not read: group posts need a signed-in account. The team should collect examples by hand (checklist §8).

### X
- Communities are topic spaces with open or restricted membership; a public account is required, and Community posts can be seen by anyone on X (S09).
- Posts can be reported to Community moderators or to X, and accounts can be blocked (S09).
- The Communities help page describes no event, date, place, RSVP or capacity feature (S09).
- X search needs a signed-in account; hashtag examples were not collected (checklist §8).

### Pantip (how people describe the problem)
- People post "looking for a partner" for sports, events and trips, often near universities (P02, P07–P09) (S05, S06).
- In the first page of S06, **8 of 10** partner requests had **no replies** (P02–P07, P10, P11). Only P08 and P09 got any.
- A student near Burapha University wants to play badminton but says they would not dare join another group without a friend (P02, Dec 2025). This matches the questionnaire, where 4 of 5 respondents said they need a friend to go along ([evidence log](evidence-log.md)).
- Beginners worry about holding others back (P03); newcomers to a city cannot find courts or partners (P06); a hiker would not go alone as a woman (P12).
- Some posters put their social-media handles in public posts so others can contact them (S05 first page), exposing contact details to anyone.
- When a request does get attention, it can work: P01 found a partner through replies.

## 6. What the research supports

- People outside the team describe the core problem in their own words: wanting to play or go somewhere but having no one to go with (S05, S06, P02–P12).
- General places to ask (Pantip, open feeds) give a poor response, and the platforms checked lack places-left, joining and student checks.
- YelloTalk shows students value a campus community, but its users complain about safety, moderation, notifications and data handling — areas JoinGun already addresses or must keep in view.

It does **not** show how many MFU students have the problem, or how often.

## 7. Ideas for the activity-meetup feature (proposals only)

Drawn from the findings above. **Not in the backlog.** The Product Owner decides whether each goes to Must, Should, Could or Won't for this build.

| Idea | From finding | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Join together with a friend, or invite a friend to an activity | 2 | Addresses "need a friend to go along"; uses the existing friends feature |
| "Beginners welcome" tag on an activity | 2, P03 | Lowers the fear of holding others back |
| Campus filter (activities near my campus) | 3 | All accounts are `.ac.th`; campus is not stored today |
| Waiting list when an activity is full | 1, 10 | Today a full activity just shows "full" |
| Repeating activity (e.g. badminton every Tuesday) | 1, P02 | Many requests are for a regular group, not a one-off |
| Show the host's past activities count | 2, 4 | Must respect F5 history privacy |

In-app chat stays out of scope (F7: Won't).

## 9. Limits

- Public pages only, read on one day; Facebook groups and X search were not read because they need an account.
- Google Play shows reviews in its own order; the 140 read are not a random sample, and keyword counts are approximate.
- Store ratings and the data-safety label are what the stores and developer show, not checked by us.
- Pantip posters are not shown to be university students, except where they say so or mention a university.
- This research complements, and does not replace, interviews with MFU students.
