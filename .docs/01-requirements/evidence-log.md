## 5. Decisions taken from this evidence

Given by the user (Tech Lead) on 2026-10-28; the Product Owner still has to confirm them. Recorded as decisions 34–36 in [DECISIONS.md](../../DECISIONS.md).

| Question | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Should the F5 default change from `friends` to `private`, given 5/5 chose "only me"? | Yes: new accounts default to "only me"; existing accounts keep their setting. |
| Does the proposal keep "friends and strangers", given 4/5 need a friend along? | Keep meeting strangers as the main idea, and say students can bring a friend along (share link, friends feed). |
| Points and rewards (2/5): park as Could or Won't for this build? | Won't for this build; out of scope in the proposal. |

## 6. What this evidence supports

- Some support for the core pain (3/5 cannot find people to join) and for the current workaround (direct chat).
- Strong preference signals for private activity history, separate data consents and short posting forms.
- It does not show how often the problem happens, how painful it is, or what students did the last time it happened.

## 7. Gaps against the W5 gate

| Gap | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| 5 respondents, all year 4 | W5 asks for at least 5 with a target of 15; no year 1–3 students or other faculties. |
| No direct quote of the pain | W5 asks for a dated log with a direct quote. The only free-text question (Q13) asks for feature ideas. |
| Most questions are preferences | W5 counts past behaviour, not "would you use…?". Only Q2 and Q5 ask about past behaviour. |
| Consent and distribution unknown | Cannot show that respondents agreed, or that they were not team members (W5 does not count teammates). |
| Path B research not yet complete | First round in [competitor-research.md](competitor-research.md) (YelloTalk, Facebook, X, Pantip; 2026-10-28) with sources and dates. Screenshots are still missing, and Facebook groups and X posts still need collecting by a signed-in team member. |

## 8. Next collection plan (draft)

Short interviews, 10–15 minutes, with students outside the team, ideally years 1–3 and other faculties. Ask consent first and record only a code, year and faculty.

1. Think of the last time you wanted to do an activity but had no one to go with. What happened?
2. What did you do to find someone? Which apps or groups did you use?
3. How did it turn out? Did you go, go alone or give up?
4. How often has that happened this semester?
5. Have you ever joined an activity with people you did not know? What made it OK or not OK?
6. When you arrange an activity with others, what goes wrong most often? Tell me about the last time.
